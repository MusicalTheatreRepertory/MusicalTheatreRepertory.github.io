Cast

Adam Hassan

Mr. Zero

Adam Hassan is a Los Angeles–based performer pursuing a B.A. in Musical Theatre at USC, with minors in Dance and Real Estate Finance. Born and raised in Dubai, he brings his Lebanese heritage into his work. His stage credits include Anastasia (Vlad), Little Shop of Horrors (Mushnik), Guys and Dolls (Big Jule), Alice in Wonderland (White Rabbit), Antigone (Tiresias), The Crucible (John Proctor), and Machinal (George H. Jones), along with ensemble roles in As You Like It and Grease. On screen, he has appeared in USC projects Michael’s Parable (Michael), Sapphos (Zeke), and 4EVER (Noah). Adam is moving to New York City following graduation to continue his work in theatre, film and music.

Niko Gephart

Shrdlu

Niko is a freshman musical theatre major attending USC’s School of Dramatic arts. He has been a performer for the last 12 years, but this is his first musical at USC! Aside from singing acting and dancing, Niko also loves hiking, swimming, writing, reading, drawing, and almost anything that involves creativity.
